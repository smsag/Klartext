# Mobile drawer

Measurements are from Obsidian 1.13 (1.13.7 where stated) in the phone emulation.

**Corner radii.** Obsidian assigns the phone its own radius tokens under `.is-mobile`. In its drawer the switcher card and its rows are 30px, the collapsed selector is a pill, and the filter field is 44px. Flattening these tokens to the theme's radius turns them into 4px rectangles with a blurred shadow above, and the active row's highlight ends hard on the selector's edge. The theme leaves them alone.

**Top band.** The theme paints every leaf `--background-primary` (WORKSPACE & LAYOUT); the drawer keeps `--mobile-sidebar-background`. The drawer's top padding is `max(--size-4-2, --safe-area-inset-top)` (Obsidian's app.css), and that padding is the only part of the drawer visible above the panel. In 1.13.7, dark mode, both drawers, it measures #222222 over the leaf's #1a1a1a; in light both are #ffffff. The theme paints exactly that padding with a `linear-gradient` of `--background-primary` sized to the same `max()`, so the selector and footer below keep the drawer's colour. Phone only: on a tablet the left drawer carries the ribbon down its side, whose top would then not match.

**Selector slack.** The collapsed selector is about 35px tall with the theme's icon, against the 52px (`--touch-size-l`) Obsidian's sizing assumes. The drawer footer (vault or file name) sits 12px under it by default; 8px is enough. The open switcher's option list reserves a strip at its bottom so its last row cannot end up under the selector; a flat 40px (from the ~35px reading of the control) is too little, because the control is a floating card whose footprint is taller than its row. The reserve is `max(--touch-size-l, 2.85 × UI font size)`, plus `--size-4-2` so the last row's bottom does not sit exactly on the selector's top (0px gap by default). The space under the footer is `--safe-area-inset-bottom` (about 34px on a phone): the home indicator, not padding.

**No reserve under drawer views.** `.workspace-drawer-inner` is a flex column, the tab container is `flex: 1`, and `.workspace-drawer-tab-options` is `position: relative`, so the collapsed selector sits below the view in normal flow (verified in 1.13). A 52px (`--touch-size-l`) reserve at the foot of every drawer view is dead panel: a plugin composer's Send button hangs two finger-widths above the selector, and Obsidian's floating-nav fade (`.workspace-leaf-content::after`, 48px, sidebar colour) paints the empty strip as a gradient band. Only the open switcher's option list needs a reserve, because it is a card floating over the drawer. A view that scrolls a list under a floating control of its own carries its own slack, as the core file list does.
